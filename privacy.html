Privacy Policy

Cover Letter AI · Effective 2 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Cover Letter AI Android app ("the app", "we") handles and why. The app is published by Ayush Srivastava. There are no user accounts, and we don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Data stored only on your device

  • Your profile: name, email, phone, location, headline, years of experience, skills and experience summary.
  • Your cover letters, together with the job details and keyword scores used to create them.

This data stays on your phone. "Delete all data" in Settings removes it. Uninstalling the app removes it too.

Data sent to our server to write a letter

When you generate a letter, the app sends your profile and the job description to our server. Our server forwards them to a third-party AI provider, which writes the letter. Your email address and phone number are not sent to the AI provider for letters. When you import a resume, the PDF is sent to our server, and its full text (including any contact details in it) is sent to the AI provider to fill in your profile. The file is then discarded.

Our server does not store your profile, resume, job descriptions or letters, and does not write them to its logs. The AI provider processes this content under its own terms to return a result.

Data our server keeps

  • App-specific device ID and IP address, with the time and type of each request (generate or resume import). We use these to count your free letters, apply daily limits and prevent abuse. They are not linked to your name or contact details.
  • Reports you submit. If you tap Report on a letter, we store the part of the letter you report (which may include your name), the reason, your optional note and your device ID, so we can review problems with AI output.
  • Basic request logs: the request type, path, result and response time. Our hosting provider may also keep standard access logs, including IP addresses.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives your app-specific device ID and your purchase information. We never see your payment details. See the Google Privacy Policy and the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Security

All data between the app and our server is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

Your choices

  • Delete all on-device data at any time from Settings → Delete all data.
  • To ask about or delete the data our server keeps (device ID, IP records or reports), email us at ayushsrivastava47@gmail.com.

Children

The app is meant for job seekers and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
